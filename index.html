Privacy Policy — MemeTrader Paper Trading

Last updated: September 18, 2026

MemeTrader Paper Trading ("the extension," "we," "our") is a browser extension for simulated, "paper" trading of meme coins while browsing GMGN, DEX Screener, Axiom, FOMO, and TERMINAL. This policy explains what data the extension handles and — just as importantly — what it does not do.

In short: the extension never handles real money, never connects to a wallet, and never places a real trade. All of your paper-trading data stays on your own device. We do not operate a server, and we do not collect, see, or receive any of your personal information.

What data is stored, and where

The extension stores the following using Chrome's built-in local storage (chrome.storage.local), entirely on your own device:

  • Your simulated paper-trading balances, positions, and trade history
  • Your simulated trading settings (fee, slippage, and gas assumptions; buy/sell presets; starting balance)
  • Pending simulated limit / take-profit / stop-loss orders
  • Panel layout preferences (position, size, collapsed/expanded state)
  • Cached price data, used briefly to reduce repeat lookups

None of this data is transmitted to us. We do not run a server, we do not have a database, and we have no way to see what you store — it lives only in your own browser's local storage, the same way a website's local settings would.

Backups

The extension lets you export your paper-trading data to a JSON file that is saved directly to your own computer, and later restore from a file you select yourself. This export/import happens entirely on your device; the file never passes through us or any third-party server.

Network requests we make

To display live token prices for your paper trades, the extension makes requests to two public, third-party price APIs:

  • DexScreener (api.dexscreener.com) — to look up a token or trading pair's current price, liquidity, and volume, using only the public chain and token/pair identifiers involved. No personal information is included in these requests.
  • CoinGecko (api.coingecko.com) — to convert a network's native currency (for example SOL or ETH) into its current USD value. No personal information is included in these requests.

These are read-only lookups of public market data. Neither service receives your identity, your paper-trading history, or any other data about you — only the token, pair, or currency identifier needed to answer the price request.

What the extension does on GMGN, DEX Screener, Axiom, FOMO and TERMINAL

The extension adds a visual overlay panel to these sites so you can place simulated trades while browsing. To do this, it:

  • Reads the page's URL to detect which token or pair you're viewing
  • On GMGN specifically, reads the publicly displayed USD price text shown on the page (the same number you can already see on screen) so simulated fills can use it when available
  • Draws its own overlay UI and, where supported, adds markers to the page's own price chart showing your simulated buy/sell points

The extension does not read, access, or transmit your account information, wallet address, wallet balance, private keys, login credentials, or any other personal or financial data from these sites. It has no ability to place a real trade, connect a real wallet, or move real funds — everything the extension does is a local simulation.

Affiliate / referral links

The extension includes optional links to GMGN, Axiom, FOMO, and TERMINAL that use our referral code (mtraderx). These links are always clearly labeled, and clicking one is entirely your choice — the extension never opens them automatically. If you sign up through one of these links:

  • You may receive a trading fee discount on that platform, as described next to the link at the time
  • We may earn a commission from that platform, at no additional cost to you

Once you click through, you leave the extension and are subject to that platform's own privacy policy and terms — we have no visibility into what happens on their site after that point.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect your name, email address, or any other personal identifier
  • We don't use analytics, trackers, or advertising services of any kind
  • We don't sell or share data with third parties, because we don't collect any to begin with
  • We don't require an account, sign-in, or wallet connection to use the extension
  • We don't set our own tracking cookies

Permissions this extension requests, and why

  • storage — to save your paper-trading data and settings locally, as described above
  • alarms — to periodically check whether any pending simulated limit/take-profit/stop-loss orders should fill, even when you don't have the relevant tab open
  • Host access to gmgn.ai, dexscreener.com, axiom.trade, fomo.family, and trade.padre.gg — to display the paper-trading overlay on those sites
  • Host access to api.dexscreener.com and api.coingecko.com — to fetch the public price data described above

Children's privacy

This extension is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children, and in any case, we do not collect personal information from any user.
